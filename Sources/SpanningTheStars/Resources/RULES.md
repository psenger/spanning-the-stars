# The Concord Codex: Rules of Spanning the Stars

Section 10 covers edge cases. Section 11 describes the optional Frontier Hazards variant.

> *The galaxy has been dark for three hundred years. Now the lanes are opening.*

## 1. Components
- 1 galaxy map of star systems and hyperspace lanes
- 45 Anchor Beacons per House, in 5 House colors
- 5 Standing markers, 1 per House
- 1 Great Span Award (10 points)
- 33 Concord Contracts
- 110 Resource Cards: 12 each of Plasma Fuel (red), Crystal Lattice (purple), Dark-Matter Ballast (black), Hull Alloy (white), Coolant Ice (blue), Bio-Gel (green), Solar Sails (yellow), Fusion Ore (orange), and 14 Quantum Cores (wild)

## 2. The Houses
2 to 5 players, each leading one chartered House: Varn Industrial (red), The Solace Trust (blue), Meridian Collective (green), House Ashkar (yellow), The Pale Fleet (black).

## 3. Preparing the Charter
1. Each House takes 45 Anchor Beacons and starts at 0 Concord Standing.
2. Shuffle the Resource Cards and deal 4 to each House.
3. Place the rest as the Supply Depot and turn 5 face up to form the Exchange. If 3 or more of the 5 are Quantum Cores, move all 5 to the Salvage Heap and turn 5 new ones.
4. Place the Great Span Award beside the map.
5. Shuffle the Contracts and deal 4 to each House. Each House secretly keeps at least 2. Returned Contracts are shuffled and placed at the bottom of the Contract deck.
6. The Concord draws lots: a random House goes first. Play passes in seat order.

## 4. Object of the Charter
Earn the highest Concord Standing by anchoring lanes between adjacent systems, fulfilling your Contracts, and building the longest continuous span. Unfulfilled Contracts cost you Standing.

A Contract is fulfilled when its two systems are joined by a continuous chain of **your own** Anchor Beacons. Rival beacons never count.

## 5. Your Turn
Take exactly 1 of these actions:
1. Requisition Resources
2. Anchor 1 Lane
3. Accept Contracts

## 6. Requisition Resources
- Take 2 cards. Each comes from the Exchange or blind from the top of the Supply Depot. Refill the Exchange immediately after each Exchange pick.
- Whenever 3 of the 5 Exchange cards are Quantum Cores, move all 5 to the Salvage Heap and turn 5 new ones.
- No hand limit.
- When the Supply Depot runs dry, shuffle the Salvage Heap into a new Depot.
- If the Depot and Salvage Heap are both empty, you cannot Requisition; Anchor a Lane or Accept Contracts instead.
- **Quantum Cores** are wild. To take a face-up Core from the Exchange, it must be your **first** card, and you take no second card. A Core drawn blind counts as a normal card; you still take 2.

## 7. Anchor 1 Lane
- A lane is a row of beacon slots between two adjacent systems. Pay as many Resource Cards as the lane has slots, all matching the lane's color. Quantum Cores replace any color.
- **Open-Space Lanes** (gray) accept a set of any **one** color.
- Place 1 Anchor Beacon on each slot and send the cards to the Salvage Heap.
- Only 1 lane per turn. Any open lane may be anchored; it need not join your network. Anchor the **whole** lane in one turn.
- You cannot anchor a lane longer than your remaining beacons.
- **High-Traffic Corridors** are twin lanes of equal length between the same two systems. One House may not hold both. With 2 or 3 Houses, once either twin is anchored, the other is closed to all.

Standing earned on anchoring:

| Lane length | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standing | 1 | 2 | 4 | 7 | 10 | 15 |

## 8. Accept Contracts
- Draw 3 Contracts (or all that remain if fewer). Keep at least 1. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.
- You can only return Contracts you just drew; accepted Contracts are binding.
- Keep Contracts secret until the Final Review. No limit on how many you hold.
- If the Contract deck is empty, you cannot take this action.

## 9. Close of the Charter Era and Final Review
When a House ends its turn with 0, 1 or 2 Anchor Beacons in stock, every House, **including that one**, takes 1 final turn. Then the Final Review:
1. Recount lane Standing.
2. Reveal Contracts: add each fulfilled value, subtract each unfulfilled value.
3. **Great Span Award:** the House with the longest continuous chain of its own beacons scores 10. All tied Houses score 10. A span may loop and revisit systems, but no beacon counts twice.

Highest Standing wins. Ties: most fulfilled Contracts, then the holder of the Great Span Award, otherwise a shared victory.

## 10. Concord Rulings
- If an Exchange reset again shows 3+ Cores, reset again. If the Depot and Heap cannot supply a spread with fewer than 3 Cores, stop resetting and leave the cards.
- If any Exchange space is empty at the end of a turn and the Supply Depot or Salvage Heap has cards, fill the empty spaces.
- If no card remains for your second draw, your turn ends with one card.
- If your first Exchange pick is refilled with a Core, you cannot take that Core as your second card.
- If you cannot Requisition, cannot Anchor any lane, and the Contract deck is empty, you pass.
- Contract value equals the shortest beacon path between its systems, plus a small bonus for very long hauls.
- If every House passes in one full round, the Charter Era closes at once and the Final Review begins.

## 11. Variant: Frontier Hazards (optional, chosen at setup)
When off, hazard lanes are ordinary colored lanes.

**Spectrum Lanes (Veil Nebula).** Nebula interference scrambles single-resource anchors. Pay one card of a **different color for each slot** (a length-3 Spectrum Lane needs 3 distinct colors). Quantum Cores fill any missing color. Standing follows the table.

**Gravity Well Lanes (Ember Belt).** The well's pull carries part of the load, but it swallows a beacon. Pay **1 fewer card** than the lane's length (matching its color as usual), and spend **1 extra Anchor Beacon** from your stock, which is lost. You need length + 1 beacons to anchor it. Standing and Great Span use the lane's printed length.
