# Changelog

This file records notable changes to Spanning the Stars. It follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Victory celebrations, held-contract map highlights, tooltips, audio cues, and illustrated in-game and macOS Help. ([#1](https://github.com/psenger/spanning-the-stars/issues/1))

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- Native macOS play for two to five Houses on one Mac. Claim hyperspace lanes, fulfill Concord Contracts, and compete for the Great Span Award.
- Five offline AI difficulty levels, from Cadet to Admiral. Choose a human or computer opponent for each House.
- The optional Frontier Hazards variant adds Spectrum Lanes and Gravity Well Lanes.
- The in-game Concord Codex, turn-by-turn privacy for local players, and distinct resource symbols.
- Autosave, five named save slots, and optional replay logs for diagnosing game issues.
- A seeded tournament runner for comparing AI difficulty levels.
